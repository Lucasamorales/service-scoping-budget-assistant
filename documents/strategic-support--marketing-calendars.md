# Marketing calendars — Strategic support

**Section:** Strategic support
**Item:** Marketing calendars
**Status code:** Y
**Status meaning:** We can do it and is within our scope
**Also known as:** marketing schedule, promotional calendar, campaign timeline, event planner

## Summary
Marketing calendars are part of the strategic support category, indicating that the central marketing team can provide them and they are included in our scope.

## What This Item Covers
Marketing calendars organize and track marketing activities, campaigns, and events over time. They help ensure that all marketing efforts are coordinated and aligned with business objectives. These calendars are typically used by the marketing department to plan and schedule promotional activities.

## What the "Y" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team can create and manage marketing calendars as part of their responsibilities. Marketing calendars are included in our scope, and no additional cost or quote is required. Requesters should expect the central marketing team to provide these calendars upon request.
