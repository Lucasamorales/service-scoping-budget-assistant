## Summary
Abandoned carts flows are email and SMS campaigns designed to recover lost sales by reminding customers of items left in their shopping carts. This item is within the scope of email marketing & SMS and can be implemented.

## What This Item Covers
Abandoned carts flows involve sending automated emails or text messages to customers who have added items to their cart but did not complete a purchase. These reminders aim to encourage customers to return to the site and finalize their orders. The central marketing team manages these campaigns, which are tailored to remind customers of uncompleted transactions.
