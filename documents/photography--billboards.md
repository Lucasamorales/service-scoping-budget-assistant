# Billboards — Photography

**Section:** Photography
**Item:** Billboards
**Status code:** On demand
**Status meaning:** This is something we need to quote
**Also known as:** outdoor advertising, large format prints, roadside displays, poster ads

## Summary
Billboards are photography-based advertisements that require a quote from the central marketing team before inclusion in a project.

## What This Item Covers
Billboards are large-scale photographic displays used for advertising purposes. They are typically placed in high-traffic areas to reach a wide audience. The item covers the process of creating and placing these billboards, which involves photography and design work tailored to the specific needs of the advertiser.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team handles billboards on a case-by-case basis, requiring a quote before inclusion in any project. Requesters should expect to provide detailed specifications and receive a cost estimate prior to proceeding with the project.

## At a Glance
- **In scope (included):** Conditional — quoted case by case
- **Delivered by:** the central marketing team, subject to quotation
- **Extra cost or quote required:** Yes
- **What to do to get it:** Submit detailed specifications and request a quote

## Likely Questions
- Q: How do I get a quote for billboards? — A: Submit detailed specifications to the central marketing team.
- Q: Are billboards included in standard photography packages? — A: No, they require a separate quote.
