## Summary
Sales & marketing managers x 2 - group calls are part of the Strategic support section and are within our scope to provide.

## What This Item Covers
This item covers a standing group call that brings the sales manager and the marketing manager together with the central marketing team on a shared bi-weekly session. It is a team-wide call, open to whoever needs to attend from either function, covering alignment on strategies and initiatives. The participants are functional managers rather than a venue's general manager.

## What the "Y" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team can organize these group calls as part of their responsibilities. The service is included within our scope without any additional cost or need for a quote. Requesters should expect to be informed about call schedules and topics in advance.
