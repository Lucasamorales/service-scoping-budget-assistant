# Signage — Photography

**Section:** Photography
**Item:** Signage
**Status code:** On demand
**Status meaning:** This is something we need to quote
**Also known as:** visual displays, signs, banners, promotional graphics, display materials

## Summary
Signage is a component of the Photography section and requires a quote before it can be provided.

## What This Item Covers
Signage includes various types of visual displays used for marketing purposes. It encompasses physical signs, banners, and promotional graphics that are designed to attract attention and communicate messages effectively. The central marketing team handles requests for signage on a case-by-case basis.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team, subject to quotation, manages requests for signage. Each request is evaluated individually, and a quote is provided based on specific requirements and availability. Requesters should anticipate receiving a detailed quote before proceeding with any project involving signage.

## At a Glance
- **In scope (included):** Conditional — quoted case by case
- **Delivered by:** the central marketing team, subject to quotation
- **Extra cost or quote required:** Yes
- **What to do to get it:** Request a quote from the central marketing team
