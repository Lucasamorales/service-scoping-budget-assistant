## Summary
Content scheduling is a part of organic social media and community management that involves organizing and planning the publication of content on social media platforms. It is managed locally by the site and operations teams.

## What This Item Covers
Content scheduling refers to the process of planning and organizing the publication of posts on social media channels. It ensures that content is distributed at optimal times to reach the target audience effectively. This item covers the tools, processes, and strategies used for scheduling posts across various organic social media platforms.
