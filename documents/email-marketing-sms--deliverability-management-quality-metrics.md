## What This Item Covers
Deliverability management involves ensuring that emails reach recipients' inboxes successfully. It includes tracking and analyzing quality metrics such as bounce rates, inbox placement rates, and complaint rates to improve email delivery performance. This is primarily for the central marketing team to monitor and manage email campaigns effectively.
