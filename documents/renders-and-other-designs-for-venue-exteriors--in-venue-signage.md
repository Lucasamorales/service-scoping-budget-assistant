# In-venue signage — Renders and other designs for venue exteriors

**Section:** Renders and other designs for venue exteriors
**Item:** In-venue signage
**Status code:** On demand
**Status meaning:** This is something we need to quote
**Also known as:** wayfinding, directional signs, venue indicators, interior signage

## Summary
In-venue signage refers to the signs and markers inside a venue that guide visitors and provide information. It falls under the category of renders and other designs for venue exteriors and requires a quote before it can be included in a project.

## What This Item Covers
In-venue signage includes various types of signs such as directional arrows, informational boards, and safety instructions. These signs are designed to help visitors navigate through the venue easily and understand the layout and features of the space.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team, subject to quotation, handles in-venue signage requests. Each case is evaluated individually, and a quote is provided based on the specific requirements. The requester should expect to receive a detailed quote outlining the costs and scope of the signage project.
