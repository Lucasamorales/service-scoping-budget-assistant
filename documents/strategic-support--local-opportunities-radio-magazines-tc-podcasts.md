# Local opportunities (radio, magazines, tc, podcasts) — Strategic support

**Section:** Strategic support
**Item:** Local opportunities (radio, magazines, tc, podcasts)
**Status code:** Local
**Status meaning:** Taken by local or operation teams
**Also known as:** local media opportunities, regional advertising options, local radio and magazine placements, community outreach programs

## Summary
Local opportunities for radio, magazines, tc, and podcasts are part of the Strategic support category and are handled by the local site and operations teams.

## What This Item Covers
Local opportunities include various forms of local media such as radio advertisements, magazine features, tc (television commercials), and podcast sponsorships. These opportunities are designed to reach a specific regional audience through local channels.

## What the "Local" Status Means for This Item
The local site and operations teams handle local opportunities, which are included in the service without additional cost or quote requirements. Requesters should expect these opportunities to be managed by the local teams and can rely on them for implementation.
