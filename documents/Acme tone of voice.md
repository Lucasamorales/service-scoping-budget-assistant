# Tone & Voice Guide (sample)

*Sample content. Invented for demonstration; replace with your client's own guide.*

## Brand content

The client is an approachable leisure brand. Everyone is welcome, from families to students to
enthusiasts, so the tone is always friendly and never exclusive.

| We are | We are not |
| ----- | ----- |
| Approachable | Exclusive |
| Upbeat | Flat |
| Clear | Technical |
| Encouraging | Preachy |

## Social tone

Positive, encouraging and relatable. Write the way a helpful member of staff would speak to a
guest at the front desk.

## Practical rules

- Short sentences and plain words.
- Lead with what the guest gets.
- One call to action per message.
- No jargon, no pressure selling.
