# Attendance (travel, accommodation, food) — Events

**Section:** Events
**Item:** Attendance (travel, accommodation, food)
**Status code:** On demand
**Status meaning:** This is something we need to quote
**Also known as:** event travel, event attendance costs, travel for events

## Summary
Staff attendance at events, including travel, accommodation, and food, is on demand and involves variable third-party costs that need a manual quote.

## What This Item Covers
Costs for people attending events on behalf of marketing — travel, stays, and meals — not the creative deliverables themselves.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
Quoted case by case. Automatic tier pricing does not apply when costs are variable.

## At a Glance
- **In scope (included):** Conditional — quoted case by case
- **Delivered by:** the central marketing team, subject to quotation
- **Extra cost or quote required:** Yes
- **What to do to get it:** Ask for a manual quote via your Account Manager

## Likely Questions
- Q: Can the bot price this automatically? — A: No — travel costs are variable.
- Q: Who approves attendance? — A: Your Country Manager / usual approval path after a quote.
- Q: Is food included? — A: Only when specified in the quote.
