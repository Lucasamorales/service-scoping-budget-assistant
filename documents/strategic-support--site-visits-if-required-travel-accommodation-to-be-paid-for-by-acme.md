**Section:** Strategic support

## Summary
Site visits and related travel and accommodation costs are part of the Strategic support category and require a quote before they can be arranged.

## What This Item Covers
Site visits involve physical visits to a location for strategic support purposes. Travel and accommodation expenses are included if required and paid for by Acme. These visits are typically arranged for specific projects or initiatives that benefit from on-site interaction.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team, subject to quotation, handles site visits and related travel and accommodation costs. These services are not automatically included and require a separate quote before they can be provided. Requesters should expect to receive a detailed quote outlining the costs involved.
