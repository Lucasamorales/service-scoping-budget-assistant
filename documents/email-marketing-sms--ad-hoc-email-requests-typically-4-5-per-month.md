## Summary
Ad hoc email requests are specific email marketing activities that require individual quotation and approval from the central marketing team. They are part of the Email marketing & SMS category and involve case-by-case assessment.

## What This Item Covers
Ad hoc email requests refer to unscheduled email campaigns initiated by various departments or teams within the organization. These requests typically occur 4-5 times a month and may vary in scope and purpose, such as promotional announcements, event invitations, or urgent notifications.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team handles ad hoc email requests on an individual basis, subject to quotation. Each request is evaluated separately, and a quote is provided before any work begins. Requesters should expect to receive a detailed proposal outlining costs and timelines.
