# Invites to like pages — Organic social media and community management

**Section:** Organic social media and community management
**Item:** Invites to like pages
**Status code:** Local
**Status meaning:** Taken by local or operation teams
**Also known as:** Promotions, page recommendations, follow requests, engagement invites

## Summary
Invites to like pages are part of organic social media and community management activities that are handled locally by the site and operations teams.

## What This Item Covers
Invites to like pages involve encouraging followers to visit and like a brand's social media pages. These invitations can be part of various social media campaigns and are aimed at increasing page engagement and reach. The item covers the process of creating and distributing such invites, typically through posts or direct messages on social media platforms.

## What the "Local" Status Means for This Item
The local site and operations teams handle invites to like pages. These activities are included in their scope without additional cost or need for a quote. Requesters should expect the local teams to manage these invitations as part of their regular responsibilities.
