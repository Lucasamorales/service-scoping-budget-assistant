## What This Item Covers
Internal decor encompasses the arrangement and appearance of physical spaces within an organization. It involves selecting and placing items such as furniture, artwork, and other decorative elements to enhance the visual appeal and functionality of the space. This item is intended for use in photography settings where aesthetics are important.
