# Influencer sourcing — Strategic support

**Section:** Strategic support
**Item:** Influencer sourcing
**Status code:** Local
**Status meaning:** Taken by local or operation teams
**Also known as:** influencer engagement, influencer identification, influencer recruitment, influencer outreach

## Summary
Influencer sourcing is a part of strategic support that the local site and operations teams handle.

## What This Item Covers
Influencer sourcing involves identifying and engaging with individuals who have influence over a specific audience to promote products or services. It is used by marketing teams to enhance brand visibility and reach target demographics effectively.

## What the "Local" Status Means for This Item
The local site and operations teams are responsible for handling influencer sourcing. This service is included in the scope without any additional cost or need for a quote. Requesters should expect the local teams to manage the process from start to finish.
