# Hoarding — Photography

**Section:** Photography
**Item:** Hoarding
**Status code:** On demand
**Status meaning:** This is something we need to quote
**Also known as:** billboards, advertising hoardings, large format displays, outdoor advertising

## Summary
Hoarding is a photography service within the Photography section that requires a quote before it can be included in a project.

## What This Item Covers
Hoarding refers to large advertising structures used for public display. It involves creating visual content for these structures, which are typically placed in high-traffic areas. The central marketing team handles requests for hoarding photography on a case-by-case basis.

## What the "On demand" Status Means for This Item
The central marketing team provides hoarding photography services subject to quotation. Requesters must obtain a quote before proceeding with the project, as this service is not automatically included and incurs additional costs.
