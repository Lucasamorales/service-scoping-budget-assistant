# Reposting relevant content — Organic social media and community management

**Section:** Organic social media and community management
**Item:** Reposting relevant content
**Status code:** Local
**Status meaning:** Taken by local or operation teams
**Also known as:** sharing relevant posts, retweeting, resharing, reposting useful content

## Summary
Reposting relevant content is part of organic social media and community management activities that are handled locally by the site and operations teams.

## What This Item Covers
Reposting relevant content involves sharing existing posts from other sources on a company's social media channels to engage with the audience and provide valuable information. It is typically performed by social media managers or community coordinators who identify and share content that aligns with the brand's message and interests of the followers.

## What the "Local" Status Means for This Item
The local site and operations teams are responsible for reposting relevant content as part of their organic social media and community management duties. The activity is included in their scope without any additional cost or need for a quote. Requesters should expect the local teams to handle this task as part of their regular responsibilities.
