# Content planning — Organic social media and community management

**Section:** Organic social media and community management
**Item:** Content planning
**Status code:** Local
**Status meaning:** Taken by local or operation teams
**Also known as:** content strategy, editorial calendar, social media planning, community engagement plan

## Summary
Content planning is a part of organic social media and community management that involves the local site and operations teams taking responsibility for it.

## What This Item Covers
Content planning refers to the process of developing a strategic approach to creating and scheduling content for organic social media and community management. It includes identifying key themes, messages, and posting schedules to engage with the audience effectively. The item is intended for use by local site and operations teams who are responsible for managing their community's online presence.

## What the "Local" Status Means for This Item
The "Local" status means that content planning is handled by the local site and operations teams. These teams are responsible for creating and implementing content strategies without requiring additional costs or quotes. The requester should expect the local teams to deliver this service as part of their standard responsibilities.
