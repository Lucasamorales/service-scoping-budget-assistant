## Summary
Audience segmentation is a feature of email marketing and SMS campaigns that allows the central marketing team to tailor messages to specific groups of customers, and it is included in our scope.

## What This Item Covers
Audience segmentation involves dividing an email or SMS list into smaller groups based on shared characteristics such as demographics, behavior, or preferences. This practice helps in delivering more relevant and personalized content to each group, enhancing engagement and effectiveness.
